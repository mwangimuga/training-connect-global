Connecting you to the right training opportunities

Training Connect Global is a referral platform. We don't run the classroom — we make sure you find the right one.

What we do

With so many training providers, formats and schedules on offer, finding the right professional development programme can take longer than the course itself. Training Connect Global exists to close that gap.

We maintain a curated catalogue of professional training programmes across data, technology, leadership, finance, HR and more. When you send an enquiry, our team reviews it and connects you with a suitable training provider and schedule — physical, online, or in-house.

Why work with us

  • One enquiry covers a wide catalogue, instead of searching provider by provider.
  • A real follow-up from a person, not an automated booking system.
  • Both open enrolment and custom in-house programmes.

How a referral works

1

You tell us what you need

Browse the catalogue or describe a custom requirement through the enquiry form.

2

We match you to a provider

Our team reviews your enquiry against available providers, schedules and formats.

3

You get confirmed details

You receive dates, cost and joining instructions directly from the matched provider.
